# Otto Remote – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

Otto Remote is the iPhone companion to Otto, a desktop app you run on your own computer. This policy covers the iOS app.

## What we collect

Nothing. Otto Remote has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and the developer runs no server that receives your data.

## How the app uses your data

- **Your computer.** When you pair the app with Otto on your computer, the pairing details (the computer's address and a pairing token) are kept on your phone in the iOS Keychain. Your messages and any photos you attach go straight from your phone to your computer over your local network or your Tailscale network. They are not sent anywhere else.
- **Camera.** The camera is used only to scan the pairing QR code shown in Otto on your computer. Nothing is recorded or stored.
- **Photos.** Photos you pick are sent only to your paired computer, as attachments to your message.
- **Emoji images.** The app downloads emoji images from the public Iconify service (api.iconify.design). Those requests contain only the emoji being drawn, nothing about you.

What Otto on your computer does with your messages (for example, sending them to an AI model provider) is set by the desktop app and its own settings, not by this iOS app.

## Removing your data

Remove a paired computer in the app, or delete the app, to erase everything it stored on your phone.

## Children

Otto Remote is not directed at children under 13.
